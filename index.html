Calendar

A schedule that won't let you double-book a room.

Every room gets its own row, every stay its own colored bar. See the whole month at a glance, and trust that what's on screen matches what's actually booked.

  • Room-by-day timeline for the full month
  • Color-coded by status — confirmed, checked-in, pending, checked-out, cancelled
  • Click any empty cell to start a new reservation pre-filled with that room and date
  • Built-in overlap check stops a room from being booked twice
Housekeeping

Every room's status, at a glance, every morning.

Housekeeping opens to today's list already sorted: which rooms need a turnover, which are mid-clean, and which are ready to sell — no walking the halls to check.

  • Three states per room: clean, in progress, dirty
  • Auto-flagged dirty the moment a guest checks out
  • Each room tagged with today's context — arriving, departing, stayover, or vacant
  • Filter the list by status to see exactly what's left to clean
